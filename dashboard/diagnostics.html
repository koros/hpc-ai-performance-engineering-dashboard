Hardware diagnostics

What the GPUs were doing while each benchmark ran: utilization, power draw, memory, temperature, clocks, step-time stability and the NCCL all-reduce probe. Use it to check that results were not limited by thermals, power caps, memory pressure or noisy trials before trusting a comparison.

Health checks

Each check runs on every condition in the current filter and reports passed, failed, not assessed (a required measurement is missing, or only one trial) and n/a (check does not apply). A ✓ needs every applicable condition assessed and passing. Click a failed count, or “not assessed”, to list those conditions.

Power draw vs utilization

Mean board power per GPU over the measured region, as a share of the card's nominal limit.

Memory headroom

Peak memory on the busiest GPU as a share of its capacity, by model.

Temperature and clocks

Hottest reading per condition against the mean SM clock (share of the platform's highest observed clock). Throttling would show as low clocks at high temperature.

NCCL all-reduce bandwidth

Bus bandwidth from the 64 MiB all-reduce probe each multi-GPU training run performs before measuring. Log scale.

Conditions

How these numbers are measured
  • nvidia-smi is sampled about every 0.1 s during each trial; values are summaries over the measured region (after warm-up). There are no stored time series.
  • Power is recorded per sample as the sum across the job's GPUs and divided here by the GPU count, so it is an average per board: one board can run hotter or closer to its limit than shown, and the power check cannot detect that. Memory and temperature are per-board maxima; utilization and SM clock are means across GPUs. Nominal limits (H100 SXM 700 W, L40S 350 W) are vendor spec values, not read from the cards.
  • Memory is the peak on the busiest GPU as nvidia-smi sees it, including PyTorch's cache; vLLM pre-allocates most of the card for its KV cache, so inference runs near capacity by design.
  • Each condition shows the mean of its trials (latest cohort, analysis-eligible), except peaks: hottest temperature, highest power and memory across trials.
  • Older L40S records predate the telemetry scope fields; their power is not normalised per GPU and they are marked legacy.
