What should I benchmark first?

Describe your workload and this page ranks every measured tuning lever by how close its evidence is to you, what it did there, and how consistently. When the nearest evidence is too far away it says so instead of guessing. It never predicts your throughput — it tells you which comparisons are worth running and what to expect from them.

Method. Every contrast row and serving-layout row in the studies export is indexed with its platform, workload parameter count and GPU count. Evidence is direct when platform, GPU count and parameter band all match (a parameter band is not an architecture match, and for inference the serving load is reported separately); nearby on the same platform within one GPU-count doubling and ~3× parameters; other platform at the same distance on different hardware; otherwise out of range and never ranked. A lever's status uses only its closest tier: worth testing when most contrasts there help (≥3% with a 95% CI excluding zero) and none hurt; avoid when most hurt and none help; no measurable gain when none help or hurt and most show no material effect (95% CI inside ±3%); inconclusive / insufficient evidence when none help or hurt and most are inconclusive or lack an interval; otherwise mixed. Memory changes and layout metrics are means without CIs in the export.
